# Support ACCB Research

ACCB is an open research benchmark for studying cognitive continuity of large language models under increasing semantic information load.

## Why support is useful

The pilot B2 study established a reproducible benchmark framework, but a confirmatory campaign requires substantially more paid inference:

- multiple independent generations per model × load condition;
- additional models and providers;
- denser semantic-load sampling;
- controlled reasoning-budget experiments;
- richer temporal and dependency scoring;
- replication by independent researchers;
- long-term archival and public reproducibility infrastructure.

Support can take several forms:

- voluntary donations;
- research grants;
- API credits;
- GPU / inference capacity;
- infrastructure hosting;
- independent replication work;
- statistical or methodological collaboration.

## Scientific independence

Financial or infrastructure support does not provide authority to alter the protocol, suppress results, select favorable outcomes, or control scientific interpretation.

Material support used in a specific experimental campaign should be disclosed publicly in the corresponding report or paper.

Where practical, future ACCB campaigns should publish a simple support-and-spend ledger showing how donated funds, credits, or compute were allocated to research execution.
